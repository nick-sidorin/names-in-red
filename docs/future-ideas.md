# Future ideas

## PvP progression

Add long-term goals to the rival journal. Players could earn achievements by
fighting different opponents and work toward a new rank through PvP.

### Achievements

Possible milestones include earning a first honorable kill, defeating a set
number of distinct rivals, or reaching a total amount of honor earned.

### Ranks

Consider three signals for progress toward the next rank.

- **Honor earned** rewards the amount of honor the player gains in PvP.
- **Honorable kills** reward the number of kills credited by the game.
- **Skirmishes won** reward the number of fights won by the player's side.

The progression metric, rank thresholds, and role of repeat encounters are
open for design. If ranks combine multiple signals, decide how a single fight
contributes when it grants both honor and a win.
